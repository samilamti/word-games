# Privacy Policy — Lexica Knights

**Effective date:** 2026-04-26

Lexica Knights ("the app") is a single-player word combat game that runs entirely on your device. This document describes what data the app handles and what it does not.

## What the app stores on your device

The app uses your browser/device's local storage to remember:

- **Game progress** — your current rack, board state, score, and turn.
- **Word disputes** — when you dispute a rejected word, the word and any optional definition you provide are saved locally so you can review your own dispute history. Storage key: `lexica_knights_disputes`.
- **Beta feedback** — text you enter through the in-app "Beta Feedback" button is saved locally. Storage key: `lexica_knights_feedback`.

This information lives on your device only. It is not synced, backed up to a server, or shared with anyone.

## What the app does not collect

- The app does **not** collect any personal information (no name, email, account, or sign-in).
- The app does **not** include analytics, tracking pixels, advertising SDKs, or third-party trackers.
- The app does **not** collect your IDFA, advertising identifier, or any device identifier.
- The app does **not** access your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, location, or other sensitive system data.
- The app does **not** share data with third parties.

## Network usage

The app does **not** make network requests for telemetry, analytics, or remote logging. It works fully offline once installed.

The only network activity that may occur:

- The web build at `https://samilamti.github.io/word-games/` is served by GitHub Pages, which is operated by GitHub, Inc. GitHub may collect standard server logs (IP, user agent, request path) for security and operations. See [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) for details. The native iOS App Store build serves the same web assets bundled inside the app and does not contact GitHub Pages at runtime.

## Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any data from children. Because no data is collected from anyone, no special handling is needed under COPPA / GDPR-K.

## Data retention and deletion

Because all stored data lives on your device, you control retention:

- **iOS:** Delete the app from your Home Screen — all locally stored game data is removed by iOS.
- **Web:** Clear your browser's site data for the page hosting the app.
